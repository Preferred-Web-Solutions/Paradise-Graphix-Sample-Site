Accessibility

Version 1 · Last updated November 9, 2026 · Template for the preview site

We built this preview with accessibility in mind and aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we did

  • Every page works with a keyboard and has a visible focus outline.
  • Text has strong contrast and the layout works at 200% zoom and on phones.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions.
  • Forms have labels and announce their status.

Known limits

Automated tools find only part of the issues, so we cannot promise full compliance. Photos and the logo are placeholders until real ones are supplied.
